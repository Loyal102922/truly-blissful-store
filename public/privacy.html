Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

TRULY BLISSFUL respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy applies to our website (trulyblissfulshop.com) and the TRULY BLISSFUL mobile app for iPhone and Android.

Information We Collect

We collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and payment-related details only for order processing and customer support purposes.

Payment information is securely processed through Stripe. TRULY BLISSFUL does not store your full payment details.

If you leave a review, submit a custom order, or contact us, we collect the name, email, message, and any photo you choose to upload.

Mobile App

Push notifications: If you allow notifications in the app, we store a device notification token so we can send you updates about new drops, restocks, and promotions. This token does not identify you by name. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's Settings.

Camera and photos: The app only accesses your camera or photo library when you choose to upload a photo for a review or custom order. We do not access your photos in any other way.

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics and our own basic product view statistics to understand how our store is used and to improve it. This information is not used to identify you personally.

How We Use Your Information

Your personal information will never be sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may use your email address to provide order updates, shipping notifications, and customer support communication.

Your Choices & Data Deletion

You can request a copy of your information, or ask us to delete it (including your app notification token), at any time by emailing support@trulyblissfulshop.com. We will respond within 30 days. Deleting the app also stops all notifications.

Updates

By using our website or app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

TRULY BLISSFUL reserves the right to update this policy at any time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
